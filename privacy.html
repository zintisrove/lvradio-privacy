LV Radio Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 26 July 2026

LV Radio has no user accounts. Favourites and recently played stations stay on your device. The app uses Firebase services for reliability, usage analytics and its public station catalogue, and Google AdMob to display a banner advertisement.

Who we are

Zintis Apps (“we”, “us”) provides the LV Radio Android application. For privacy questions or requests, contact zintis.rove@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

Your favourite stations, recently played stations, review counters and related preferences are stored locally. We do not upload these items to our servers. You can remove them by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app.

Firebase services

LV Radio uses the following Google Firebase services:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase processes an app-instance identifier, approximate location derived from a masked IP address, app/device information, lifecycle events and interactions such as starting playback or changing a favourite. We use this to understand and improve the app. Advertising ID collection and ad-personalisation signals are disabled.
  • Firebase Crashlytics processes crash logs, relevant app state, device and operating-system information, installation identifiers and diagnostics so we can find and fix stability problems.
  • Firebase Remote Config processes app/device metadata, locale, time zone, country and a Firebase installation identifier to deliver app configuration.
  • Cloud Firestore supplies the public radio-station catalogue. LV Radio does not use it to upload your favourites, listening history or other user-created data.

These services encrypt data in transit and process it under Google’s Firebase privacy and security terms. We do not sell personal information.

Advertising and your privacy choices

LV Radio displays one banner advertisement using Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process and share an IP-derived approximate location, app interactions, diagnostics, the Android advertising ID, app set ID and other applicable device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud-prevention purposes. This data is encrypted in transit. Google describes its processing in the Google Privacy Policy.

Where required, Google’s User Messaging Platform asks for your advertising privacy choices before the app requests ads. Depending on your choice and region, AdMob may show personalised, non-personalised or limited ads. When required, you can review or change your selection from More options → Privacy choices in the app. You can also reset or delete the Android advertising ID in your device’s privacy settings.

Radio streams and external links

When you select a station, your device connects directly to that station’s stream provider. As with any internet request, that provider can receive technical data such as your IP address, device network information and request time. Each independent provider controls its own processing and retention. External pages opened from the app are governed by their own privacy policies.

Purposes and legal basis

We process technical and usage data to provide the station catalogue and playback, maintain security and reliability, diagnose failures, improve LV Radio and fund the service with advertising. Where applicable, we rely on performing the service you request, your advertising consent, and our legitimate interests in operating and improving a secure, reliable app.

Retention and deletion

Local app data and applicable consent choices remain until you remove or reset them. Firebase and advertising data is retained according to the settings and policies of our Firebase, Google Analytics and Google AdMob services and only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, subject to security and legal requirements. You may contact us to request access, correction, restriction, objection or deletion of data associated with you. Because the app has no account, we may need information from you to identify a relevant app installation and may not always be able to link pseudonymous records to a person.

Children

LV Radio is a general-audience radio utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

International processing and security

Service providers may process data outside your country. Appropriate contractual and technical safeguards apply where required. We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or legal requirements change. The latest version and effective date will always be published on this page.
